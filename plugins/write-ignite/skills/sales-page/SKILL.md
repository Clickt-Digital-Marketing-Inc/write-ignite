# Sales page

Turn the user's actual offer into persuasive, original sales copy. This is an unofficial application of the core Write & Ignite resources by Alex Cattoni / The Copy Posse, with additional evidence and delivery controls. It is not the course, its official AI assistant, or a transcript of its videos. See [source coverage](references/sources.md) when attribution or coverage matters.

**Always start with the [anti-slop foundation](references/anti-slop.md), including its bundled rules and evaluation.** Apply it while planning and drafting, then run it again before returning copy. It is part of every mode, including short edits, rather than an optional cleanup step. Preserve the user's actual voice, point and supported details.

## Choose the requested result

| User needs | Read | Produce |
|---|---|---|
| An offer brief or stronger positioning | [Offer](references/offer.md) | A grounded offer brief with feature-to-benefit mapping and material gaps |
| An outline | Offer + [Structure](references/structure.md) | The argument for the page, with evidence and section decisions |
| A complete sales page or rewrite | Offer + Structure + [Editing](references/editing.md) | Original copy followed by a concise review and unresolved facts |
| A critique or improvement pass | Structure + Editing; Offer if positioning is unclear | Prioritized findings tied to exact passages, with specific fixes |

For a detection-only request, follow the foundation's detect mode: report named patterns, exact quoted passages and brief fixes, without rewriting or scoring the draft or guessing its authorship.

For an end-to-end writing request, work through briefing, structure, drafting and review in one flow. Do not stop for approval at each stage or return only an outline when a full draft was requested. For a narrow change, keep the work narrow; a hero rewrite does not require rebuilding the page. Match the user's requested format and length.

## Establish the offer and evidence

Start from the conversation and the supplied files, product pages, research, brand guide and existing copy. Read relevant sources before making claims about them. Reuse available facts instead of asking the user to enter them again. If a URL cannot be read, state that limitation; do not imply it was audited.

Establish the product/service, audience and primary problem, desired action, solution mechanism, actual deliverables, differentiation, evidence, price/currency/payment terms, purchase/delivery process, and brand voice. Ask only for missing facts that would materially change the draft. Continue useful writing around non-blocking gaps. When the core audience or offer is unknown, a focused question is more useful than an invented page.

Separate four kinds of information in working notes:

- **Provided fact:** supplied by the user or a named product source; record the source without implying independent verification.
- **Verified evidence:** checked research, documentation, quote or data, with an identifiable source and any relevant date.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposed pain point, benefit, objection or positioning angle that needs confirmation.
- **Missing / not applicable:** distinguish an unknown price from a confirmed absence of discounts, bonuses or deadlines.

Keep these notes separate from visitor-facing copy. A brief request may need only a few notes; a full draft benefits from a compact claim/source/status table. Preserve exact approved prices, quantities, eligibility, refund terms and dates. Do not import facts from course examples or other clients.

## Build the argument

Use the ten offer lenses to understand what makes the purchase worthwhile. Explain the general solution before introducing the branded product; separate functional benefits from the emotional effect of those benefits. Treat AI brainstorming as hypotheses, never as customer research.

Use the sixteen structural functions as a reasoning guide. They are not sixteen mandatory visible headings. Merge or shorten sections to suit the offer, traffic awareness and requested page length. In the outline or review notes, mark absent promotions, bonuses, scarcity or policies as not applicable when confirmed absent; do not invent them to fill the formula. Preserve a concise decision trail when adapting the structure.

For a simple offer or short page, the general solution and named product can fit in one sentence or paragraph. Preserve the logical order without explaining the same mechanism repeatedly. Each section should add a buyer-relevant detail or answer a different question; do not pad to a requested approximate word count.

Select the CTA around the user's actual conversion event: buying, booking, requesting a quote, joining a waitlist, or another specified action. A consultation page should not promise instant product access. Explain the real next step and any commitment involved.

## Write in the user's voice

Use the brand's supplied vocabulary, degree of formality, locale and audience knowledge. Without a voice guide, default to clear conversational language and state that assumption briefly in the notes. Apply the method without copying Alex's persona, catchphrases, examples, or another brand's copy.

Before rewriting, identify the voice traits worth preserving. Make the minimum effective edit for the requested scope. Remove generic openings, invented profundity, stock contrast formulas, vague importance claims, repetitive rhythms and decorative formatting. Apply the portability test: if a sentence could fit an unrelated product unchanged, give it supported detail or cut it. Never invent a number or customer anecdote to make a sentence concrete.

Draft a connected argument with concrete details, section openers and transitions. Avoid displaying framework labels as website headings unless they suit the brand. Add CTA copy and destinations or explicit destination gaps where useful. Keep editorial notes outside the copy, except an unmistakable placeholder where omitting it would hide a material unknown.

Prefer supported specificity to larger promises. Do not fabricate testimonials, customer language, research, credentials, results, comparison prices, price history, refund guarantees, security assurances or urgency. A founder's estimate is not measured performance. If evidence is missing, narrow the claim, omit it, or mark what is needed. General human empathy does not require inventing a customer quote.

For value comparisons, use like-for-like, sourced costs and state relevant limitations. Show a regular-versus-sale price only when both are real. Use scarcity only when there is a genuine limit and a real explanation. Offer bonuses only when included. These safeguards are practical adaptations, not claims that every course section applies to every business.

## Edit, review and deliver

Apply the relevant editing passes and check the full page for consistent promises, prices, included items, tone, terms, CTA action and post-purchase expectations. Review section openers on their own to check whether skimmers can understand the offer. Do not assign an arbitrary conversion score or imply a conversion lift has been measured.

Run the bundled [no-ai-slop evaluation](references/no-ai-slop/eval.md) before returning a draft or rewrite, using the context adaptations in the foundation. Fix failed applicable checks and re-read the changed passages. Keep this self-check internal unless requested; report unresolved substantive issues. For a rewrite, add a short account of what changed and why, including any restructuring. Do not remove genuine uncertainty, useful humor or clear personal cadence to make every sentence equally polished.

Check the full page for repeated propositions as well as repeated words. Cut instructions that merely tell the reader which point to notice; state the actual implication or decision instead. A heading, paragraph and recap should not each repeat the same feature explanation unless the repetition serves a clear reading need.

For critiques, return a short priority table: passage/section, issue, consequence for the reader, concrete revision, and required evidence if any. A missing optional section is not automatically a defect. For rewrites, include the revised copy, not just commentary.

For a full draft, normally deliver:

1. The complete page copy in the requested format.
2. Brief notes on material assumptions, intentionally omitted functions, and unresolved facts.
3. A concise review that distinguishes copy checks completed from checks requiring a rendered page or human review.

If the user asks for files, save these in the agreed project location using descriptive names. Do not write client work into the plugin's own installation. Avoid creating unnecessary extra documents for a small task.

Do not report a spoken read-through, measured readability grade, mobile layout check, working checkout, or successful CTA test unless actually performed. For a rendered page, inspect the real page and relevant destination before reporting runtime behavior; never complete an order as a copy check. Writing and reviewing do not authorize publishing, sending messages, or changing checkout terms. Preserve any explicit publication authority already granted by the user.
