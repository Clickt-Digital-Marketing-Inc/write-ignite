# Eleven editing passes

Use this reference in `review` mode or after drafting. Review the buyer's route through the argument as well as individual sentences. Return actionable changes with the affected passage or section, the reason, and a proposed revision. Preserve factual meaning, approved terms, and the user's brand voice.

Apply the mandatory [anti-slop foundation](anti-slop.md) alongside these passes, including its bundled rules and evaluation. Let the course define what the page needs to communicate; use the foundation to prevent stock rhetoric and preserve the writer's voice. Respect detection-only requests without rewriting.

## Course-derived passes

1. **Read aloud.** Listen for awkward phrasing, unnatural language, abrupt joins, and words that impede delivery. If no human or audio review has occurred, perform a text-based rhythm check and label it that way. Leave actual read-aloud review unverified.
2. **Add transitions.** Check that each idea gives the reader a reason to continue to the next. Add concise connections where logic, sequence, or cause is unclear. Avoid inserting empty connective phrases into every paragraph.
3. **Cut repetition.** Remove repeated ideas and explanations that do not improve understanding or confidence. Keep repetition that serves a purpose, such as a clear purchase recap, while checking that it does not introduce conflicting terms.
4. **Use active voice.** Prefer clear actors and direct verbs when they make the message easier to understand. Preserve passive constructions when the actor is irrelevant or the sentence is clearer that way.
5. **Break text walls.** Create manageable sentences and paragraphs. The checklist's loose guide is roughly 15–20 words per sentence and one or two sentences per paragraph. Treat this as an editing aid; vary the rhythm and retain necessary precision.
6. **Apply selective bolding.** Emphasize the few phrases that help a scanning reader understand the argument. Do not bold whole passages or let styling substitute for clear wording. Check the rendered effect separately when a page is available.
7. **Strengthen section openers.** Let early section headings connect with the buyer's experience; after the offer appears, give more attention to concrete decision information. Read headings alone: they should communicate a coherent account of the offer and its relevance.
8. **Proofread.** Check spelling, punctuation, grammar, names, numbers, capitalization, and consistency. Reconcile prices, payment-plan totals, deadlines, offer contents, policy terms, and CTA labels against their evidence sources. Flag a contradiction that cannot be resolved from available facts.
9. **Check mobile formatting.** Inspect the rendered page at an appropriate mobile viewport when browser access is available. Look for dense sections, awkward wraps, overflow, obscured controls, and hard-to-use CTAs. A text-only review can suggest fixes but cannot verify the mobile experience.
10. **Check readability.** Use Hemingway or another disclosed measurement tool when available. The course suggests approximately grade seven for a general audience; adjust to the actual audience and required terminology. Report the tool and measured result only when a measurement was performed. Otherwise label the assessment qualitative.
11. **Check CTA placement.** Review the opportunities above the fold; after the lead-in and prescription; after the offer breakdown; after value and bonuses; after purchase details; and at the bottom. Match each CTA to its reader context and a clear destination. Repeated buttons must present consistent offer terms.

## Practical plugin adaptations and safeguards

Before polishing unsupported language, mark the claim and identify the evidence needed. A clearer sentence is not stronger evidence. Check that the solution category precedes the branded product, that functional benefits remain distinct from emotional possibilities, and that optional promotions, bonuses, proof, or guarantees were not invented to fill a template.

Review every testimonial, quantitative claim, comparative statement, price anchor, valuation, and scarcity statement against a linked source. Distinguish a customer's exact words from paraphrase. Preserve uncertainty and conditions that materially affect the buyer's decision. Do not certify conversion performance from an editorial review.

Report three separate results:

- **Copy reviewed:** passages examined, substantive edits, unresolved factual questions, and applicable passes completed.
- **Runtime reviewed:** the actual URL or build inspected, viewport or device, destinations exercised, and observed results. Include only checks performed.
- **Still unverified:** human read-aloud review, missing sources, readability measurement, mobile behavior, payment or checkout flow, or other relevant checks that remain open.

CTA presence in a draft does not prove that the button exists, is visible above the fold, opens the right destination, or completes checkout. Do not submit a purchase to validate copy. Keep absent proof or inapplicable commercial elements as explicit gaps or N/A with reasons. Finish with the highest-impact corrections and a clear statement of what the review establishes.
