# Brief review

**Copy reviewed:** The draft follows the supplied British English voice and links both CTAs to `/start`. Product functions, price, cancellation, setup charge and trial terms were checked against the user’s brief, which is the source for these facts. Emotional relief is framed as a possibility. The revision was checked for repeated propositions and the named anti-slop patterns. This remains an editorial check, not a conversion prediction.

**Decisions and gaps:** The short page combines the solution and product introduction, then addresses the client relationship and purchase terms. Founder history and comparative value claims are omitted because none were supplied. Promotions, bonuses, scarcity, testimonials and guarantees are not applicable to this brief. The trial-to-paid transition was not supplied, so the copy does not state whether billing begins automatically or requires a separate choice. Confirm that detail before publication if it needs to appear on the page.

**What changed:** Combined the category and product introduction, removed the redundant process section and its interpretive reader guidance, and cut the closing recap. Kept the opening voice, client context and complete offer terms. The page is shorter than the roughly 450-word target because additional length would repeat the small set of supplied facts.

**Runtime reviewed:** None; this is a text draft. Human read-aloud review, measured readability, mobile layout and the actual `/start` flow remain unverified.
